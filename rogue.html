Rogue

Opportunistic Strike

Level 5

Immediately after an ally hits a creature within 5 feet of you with an attack, you can take a Reaction to make one melee attack against that creature.

Reliable Talent

Level 7

When you make an ability check using a skill or tool with which you have proficiency and roll a 9 or lower on the d20, you can treat the roll as a 10.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Arcane Trickster

Magical Ambush

Level 6

If you have the Invisible condition when you cast a spell on a creature, it has Disadvantage on any saving throw it makes against the spell on the same turn.

Improved Magical Ambush

Level 9

You can also use your Sneak Attack when you hit a creature with a spell attack.

The extra damage is the same type as the spell’s damage. If the spell deals more than one damage type, you choose which type the extra damage uses.

Assassin

Fatal Precision

Level 6

During the first round of each combat, you can deal maximum damage with your Sneak Attack dice instead of rolling them.
