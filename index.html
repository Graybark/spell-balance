Notes

  • 1st-level slots: two at character level 1; three from level 2 onward.
  • 2nd–5th-level slots: one when unlocked; a second at the next character level; then capped at two.
  • 6th–9th-level slots: one when unlocked, with a second 6th-level slot at character level 19 and a second 7th-level slot at character level 20.
  • New spell levels unlock every other character level, through 9th-level spells at character level 17.
